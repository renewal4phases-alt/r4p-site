What Happens Next?

After you submit the form, R4P can review the information and determine the best next step for follow-up and support.

1. Review Your application is reviewed to understand your current needs and situation.
2. Contact If appropriate, someone can follow up to ask questions, clarify needs, or discuss possible support.
3. Support Path The goal is to connect each person to the most appropriate starting point inside the R4P model.
