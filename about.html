About R4P

Restoring Lives. Strengthening Communities.

Renewal Four Phases exists to create a structured path from instability to healing, preparation, and a new beginning.

Our Why

In Tacoma and across Washington, too many people are caught in a cycle of homelessness, instability, and limited options. Emergency help matters, but emergency help alone is not enough. People need a real pathway—one that addresses immediate need while also building toward long-term independence.

Renewal Four Phases was created around that idea. The goal is to provide more than short-term relief. R4P is meant to help individuals move through a structured process of stabilization, renewal, preparation, and transition so they can rebuild with dignity, purpose, and support.

The vision behind R4P is not only shelter, but restoration: helping people reconnect to safety, community, practical opportunity, and a future that feels reachable again.

Our Commitment

Dignity Every person deserves to be treated with respect, compassion, and humanity.
Empowerment Support should help people build capacity, not remain stuck in crisis.
Community Healing becomes stronger when people are supported, seen, and connected.
Structure Clear phases create momentum, accountability, and a path that people can actually follow.

Who We Serve

R4P serves individuals and families experiencing homelessness, housing instability, or life-disrupting hardship. The program is designed for people who need both immediate support and a longer-term pathway forward.

That may include people who need help stabilizing after crisis, people trying to reconnect to housing and employment, or people who simply need a system that supports them through more than one moment of emergency.

The long-term goal is to build a model that helps participants not only survive, but move into sustainable living with stronger support systems, clearer planning, and a renewed sense of direction.
