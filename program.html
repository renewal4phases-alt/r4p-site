The Four-Phase Model

R4P is structured around four progressive stages designed to move individuals toward long-term stability and independence.

1. Stabilization

Establish immediate safety, remove barriers, and create foundational structure.

2. Development

Build discipline, consistency, practical skills, and personal accountability.

3. Empowerment

Apply structure and learned systems within real-world conditions and responsibilities.

4. Sustainability

Maintain long-term independence through continued structure, progression, and measurable growth.
